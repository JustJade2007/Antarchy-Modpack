Antarchy Modpack

A feature-rich Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge modpack combining expansive colony building, industrial automation, deep RPG progression, and modern warfare mechanics. Key Features
🏛️ Colony Building & Economy

MineColonies Ecosystem: Build and manage custom settlements with dedicated add-ons for unique architectural styles and streamlined citizen management.

Player-Driven Commerce: Trade, open shops, and build wealth using Lightman's Currency and Bountiful task boards.

⚙️ Automation, Technology & Magic

Kinetic Engineering: Automate production lines, trains, and mechanical contraptions using Create and Create Deco.

Resource Generation: Cultivate renewable resources with Mystical Agriculture and process materials using Iron Furnaces and Cyclic.

⚔️ Progression, Combat & Warfare

Deep RPG Systems: Unlock custom stats, high-tier enchants, and mob farms with the Apotheosis suite.

Modern Combat: Arm yourself with firearms and tactical systems from Superb Warfare, complemented by Simply Bows, Artifacts, and Relics.

Exploration & Navigation: Traverse expanded terrain from Biomes O' Plenty using Xaero's Minimap & World Map, Nature's Compass, and Explorer's Compass.

🔒 Base Defense & Security

Fortified Bases: Secure your perimeter with keypads, lasers, and reinforced blocks from SecurityCraft.

Land Management: Claim chunks, prevent griefing, and organize factions through FTB Chunks and FTB Teams.

📦 Storage & Aesthetics

Mass Storage: Manage inventories using Tom's Simple Storage, Sophisticated Storage, and upgraded Sophisticated Backpacks.

Interior Design: Customize builds with Refurbished Furniture, Handcrafted, Chipped, and Macaw’s Lights.

🎙️ Immersion & Quality of Life

Proximity Voice: Communicate seamlessly using Simple Voice Chat paired with Sound Physics Remastered.

Streamlined Survival: Includes Waystones, Corpse retrieval, Veinminer, Tree Harvester, and Carry On.
